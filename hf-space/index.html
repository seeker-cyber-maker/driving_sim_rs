Help & tutorial ↗
About & feedback

AI Training Story: Driving Around is a standalone controller workshop inspired by AI Learns To Drive. Train a population, evaluate an unchanged driver, and inspect its measured replay. You or an agent can operate the experiment tools; the opening question is a challenge to investigate through matched comparisons.

Simulator by Zorro909; interface and hosting by TiGa. Inspired by the game by ActivationFunction. Built-in tracks are reconstructed replicas. For supported conditions, the lab checks the run’s final fitness policy (or frozen input) with a bounded fresh replay. It predicts conditions only and does not award or accumulate original-game stars; opponent races and other unsupported conditions remain unchecked.

Uploaded files are used for the requested run. Results are held briefly in application memory and deleted after verified download or expiry. Browser recipes contain settings only. Read the data policy.

The map shows the road and walls from native track-part geometry. An optional suggested route gives a rough reference alongside the car’s recorded path. Record a short CPU drive to watch an unchanged policy using real vehicle positions. A new recording can differ from an earlier training run. Native control commands and consumed sensor inputs appear in the black box. Ray-hit positions and neural activations are not recorded.

What does this recording show?
A single fresh CPU drive records native state, raw and normalized control commands, and the exact cached sensor inputs every 0.1 seconds. Physics and policy decisions run at 60 Hz. Playback starts paused and shows measured poses by default; optional pose smoothing only interpolates between adjacent measured samples. It does not simulate the car. Black-box values always remain at an actual recorded sample. Ray-hit positions, skid marks and hidden-layer activations are not recorded. This is a selected-policy CPU replay, including for GPU-trained policies; it is not a recording of the original training population.
The recording remains in this page until replaced, cleared by a changed selection, or the page is closed. Use Download recording to keep its measured samples.
